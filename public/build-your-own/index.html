04Questions

Common questions.

Can I take the two agents together?

Yes. The price is for each agent. Two agents cost RM1998 for the setup and RM598 each month. The two agents work in the same Telegram group.

Do I install software?

No. You use Telegram. We install the agent on a private server. We connect the agent to your Telegram group.

Who can speak to the agent?

Each person in your Telegram group can speak to the agent. You control the members of the group.

Is my data safe?

Your agent runs on a private server. We do not share your data with other clients. We do not use your data to train a model.

Can you change the agent after the setup?

Yes. Send a message with the change. The monthly fee covers the change.

How do I stop the service?

Send an email. We stop the service at the end of the month. We give you your data.
